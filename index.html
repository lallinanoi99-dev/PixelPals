    chapter 08 · faq

    Questions from the playground.

    1. 01Is it free?

      Yes. Hatching costs nothing — you only pay a tiny bit of gas on BOT Chain. Feeding, playing and sleeping are tiny transactions too.

    2. 02Can my pal die?

      Never. Pals get a little sad if you forget them, but they always wait for you.

    3. 03What happens if I forget?

      Miss one day and your pal feels “okay”. Miss two and a little rain cloud appears. One full care day — feed, play and sleep — makes it happy again. Its stage never goes down.

    4. 04How many can I have?

      Each wallet can hatch up to 3 pals. There are 677 eggs in total.

    5. 05Where is the art stored?

      Inside the contract. The sprites are stored as compact pixel data, and the contract draws the SVG every time someone asks — no IPFS, no server.

    6. 06Can I sell or gift my pal?

      Yes — it’s a normal ERC-721 NFT. The new owner keeps caring for it, and its care days stay with it.

    7. 07Is the randomness fair?

      Traits are rolled with on-chain block data. That is fine for cosmetic traits, but it isn’t secure randomness — nothing valuable depends on it.
